The Journal header

Today's Journal header stacks three things: the wordmark with four buttons, then a grey Journal | Lists pill across the whole width, then filter chips when a filter is on. The pill is the only flat grey control left in a screen where the tab bar and the buttons are glass, which is why it feels unrelated to the nav.

Every phone below is live. Tap Journal or Lists to switch, and scroll inside it to see the header step away and come back with the glass bar from build 203.

What I found on Mobbin

  • The title is the switch. Instagram (Following / Favorites), Fable and Sora put a small chevron on the title and open a glass menu. Beli does the same with Restaurants ⌄.
  • Two titles, side by side. Linear (Issues / Projects) and Apple Music's Library use plain words, the current one bright and the other dimmed. No pill at all.
  • Glass chips under the title. ElevenReader (For you / Following / Recents) and Superlist use small capsules that hug their words, made of the same material as the tab bar.
  • Lists as their own place. Beli gives "Your Lists" a tab. Letterboxd keeps lists on the profile, not the diary.

TodayBuild 203

A. "Journal ⌄" opens a menu

One row. The wordmark gives way to "Journal" in Young Serif, the same title Feed and Saved already have, with a small chevron. Tapping it opens iOS's own glass menu with Journal and Lists, and the title becomes "Lists ⌄". The pill is gone. The cost is that Lists are hidden until you tap the title, so the first-run Lists tip would point at the title instead.

B. "Journal Lists" as two titlesRecommended

One row. Both words are titles in Young Serif; the one you are on is full ink and the other is dimmed. Tap either to switch. Lists stay in sight, there is no pill, and it reads like Feed and Saved's titles. To fit both words and the glass group on one row, the bug moves off the Journal to the You tab's header (it only shows in TestFlight builds, so real users never see it).

C. A glass switch like the tab bar

Keeps today's layout but rebuilds the switch from the tab bar's own parts: a small Liquid Glass capsule that hugs "Journal" and "Lists", with the same raised pill the tab bar uses for the selected tab. When a filter is on, its chips sit to the right on the same row. It is the smallest change, and still two rows.

D. Lists move to the You tab

The Journal becomes only your entries: "Journal" and its buttons, nothing under it. Your lists become a section of the You tab, with "See all" opening today's grid. This changes where Lists live, which is a bigger product call than the header, so I'm showing it for completeness rather than recommending it.
