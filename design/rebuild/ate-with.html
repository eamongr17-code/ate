Ate With

You tag who you ate with. They hear about it, open a copy of the visit with the same place and the same dishes, slide their own scores, and post it as their own entry. One key in the composer, one row type in one notifications list, nothing new in the tab bar.

Decline from his entry

On an entry she is tagged in, the ••• actions sheet gains one row, Remove me, only for her. It asks once in the native dialog. His entry drops "with @jess"; he is not notified. Block here also clears every tag between them.

The invite link, without the app

ate.app/w/‹code› opens a plain web page on the coral Share ground: Eamon's printed receipt for the visit, exactly as Share renders it, titled "@eamon ate with you at Tipo 00", and the standard Download on the App Store badge. Nothing else: no form, no sign-up on the web.

With the app installed, the same link is a universal link and opens the scoring sheet directly.

Installed from the page: Welcome, then the handle step, then the scoring sheet opens on its own. iOS does not carry a link through an install by itself, so the app re-reads it once on first launch (the copied link on the pasteboard, or the page's smart banner link). If that fails, the tag still lands in her list once she signs up with the phone number or email the invite was sent to; otherwise the link simply opens again from the message.

If the entry was never posted or was deleted, the page shows only the badge.

Friction budget

  • Tagger, not tagging: zero added taps; one more disc in the capsule.
  • Tagger, one person: three taps (key, row, pill). Two people: four.
  • Tagged, three dishes: tap the push, three drags, tick. Five actions to a printed receipt; no typing, no place search, no dish matching.

Needs from the backend

  • entry_companions (entry, person or invite code, state: pending, posted, dismissed, declined). RLS: only the tagger writes; the tagged person can only change their own row's state; a block on either side refuses the insert.
  • A notifications read listing pending companion rows for me, newest first, with place, date and dish names.
  • Push: an edge function fired after the sorter finishes, sending to registered APNs tokens. Needs an APNs auth key (free with the developer account) and a device-token table.
  • Create from a tag: post an entry whose dish lines are given (not sorted from words), dated the tagger's visit, with optional words sorted and merged without duplicating listed dishes.
  • Invite codes: reserve on share, bind on post, claim on sign-up; expire unbound codes after a week.
  • Matching visit: same place within a day, for Already logged and for linking reciprocal tags.
