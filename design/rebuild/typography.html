What else changes, and what can't

Fixed in the same sweep

  • Dish and list names on shelf cards, photo tags and list tiles move to Young Serif, so a dish's name is never in two fonts.
  • Scores all use one weight (700) with even-width digits, instead of a mix of 800 and 600.
  • “with @jess” on the Ate-with sheet used a faked bold. It gets the real semibold.
  • Settings › Appearance showed its value (System / Light / Dark) in iOS's font. It gets the sans.

Stays in iOS's own font (SF Pro)

  • Long-press menus, ••• menus, alerts, delete confirmations, swipe actions, the calendar date picker, the keyboard and the status bar.
  • Apple draws these itself and gives apps no way to change their font. This is a big part of why DM Sans is the pick: it sits right beside SF Pro, so the seam disappears. Bricolage's quirky shapes are what made those spots look random.

Licence and cost

  • DM Sans and Instrument Sans: SIL Open Font License, free to bundle in an iOS app and in shared images. One credit line in the app's notices.
  • SF Pro: free, but only as iOS's own font, and its licence is murky for images posted to Instagram. One variable file for DM Sans adds about 240 KB to the app.
