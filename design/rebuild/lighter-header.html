A lighter header for Journal, Feed and Saved

Option B on all three tabs. The header is Liquid Glass and slides away while you scroll down, then comes back as soon as you scroll up or tap the clock. On Journal, the Journal/Lists switch is part of the header: it stays pinned with the title and hides with it. Each phone is drawn at real iPhone size and scrolls.

At rest the page shows no band or edge at all. The glass edge under the bar appears only once something has scrolled under it, which is what stops the lighter band from builds 92 to 95 coming back. The bar, its buttons, the switch and the edge are the system's own Liquid Glass and scroll-edge effect, with nothing custom painted on top. Feed is shown as it is in the beta, with no buttons in its bar.
