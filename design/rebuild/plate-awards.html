Plate awards, round 6

Nineteen plates and a glass

Every award is a different piece from a real tradition, each with its own shape, glaze and pattern. Numbers appear only where the number is the award: dishes logged, the scores you gave, and 10 saves. Every other award carries a symbol instead, and nothing on the front is a word. Spin them, turn them over, and tap any piece in the set to load it. Switch to Dark to see them on a dark screen.

Drag the plate to spin it.

The research

Each plate borrows from one tradition. The order roughly follows how special the tradition is: humble restaurant ware for your first dish, and Sèvres porcelain with raised gold for your 500th. Numbers are centred on the shape you see, not on the type box, so a 1 or a 0.5 sits in the true middle of the plate.
