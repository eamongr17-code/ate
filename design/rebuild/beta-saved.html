Open your own

A sheet, not a page, since there are no reviews to show. The link opens the video. "I ate it" starts a review with the dish and place already filled in; once it prints, the dish leaves Saved for your Journal.

Photos and thumbnails are colour stand-ins. Phones show the light default; dark follows the existing ink tokens. Sharing from TikTok and Instagram into this list is its own mockup, next.

Your call

"I ate it" removes the dish from Saved. It's in your Journal from then on, as a review. The alternative is keeping it in Saved, ticked.
