Diet

You pick what you always eat once. Every dish you log is then saved with it, without you tagging anything. You don't see those tags on your own entries, but everyone else does. A dish is labelled for everyone once enough people mark it.

All phones are real iPhone size. The first two are live: tap the chips and the rows.

The rules underneath

SavingWhen an entry is saved, every dish gets your diet's codes, unless you turned one off for that dish. Vegan saves as VG and also counts as V and DF.
ShowingOn your own screens, codes that come from your diet are hidden. Anyone else sees every code.
Changing dietThe change only applies to dishes logged afterwards. Past entries are left alone.
Dish labelA dish is labelled once at least 3 different people have marked that code in the last 12 months and nobody with that diet has turned it off on the dish. Below 3 it shows "Ordered by N … eaters" instead.
Gluten free wordingThe GF label means "people who eat gluten free marked it", not a guarantee. We never call a dish safe.
