Lists as playlists

A list is a playlist of dishes. The shelf becomes a grid of covers made from your own photos, and a list opens like an album: the cover big, the name under it, then the dishes in your order.

New and empty

A fresh list's cover is its accent with the name on it, until its first photo lands. Share appears once there's a dish. The picker still rises on its own the first time.

What changes

  • Replaced: the colour-blocked list card (shelf) and the large-title header on the list page.
  • New kit part: AteListCover (mosaic, single photo or accent with name), used by the shelf tile, the shelf row and the list header.
  • Unchanged: the data and server, the picker, drag to reorder, swipe to remove with Undo, the list receipt, Rename and Delete.
  • Contract: the mosaic is a new photo treatment (square, untilted, 2×2). It needs your OK alongside the look.
