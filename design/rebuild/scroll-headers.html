What changes

Under the bar

Now: tab roots fade content into a soft frost. Pushed pages (list, place, dish, tag, person, entry, settings) put a flat block of ground behind the bar with a hard bottom edge. New: the same soft frost everywhere. The page's own colour at 82% over a blur, easing out over 31pt.

The page's name in the bar

Now: it's in DM Sans 17 and appears at a different point on each page. On the list page that's 60pt after the cover; elsewhere it's half a row. New: Young Serif 18, the same face as the big title, appearing the moment the big title has gone under the bar. A short fade up, on every page.

Stays the same

Tab roots keep their header that steps away as you scroll down and comes back as you scroll up. The back button and glass buttons never move. The entry page keeps the author in its bar.
