The sheet holds still until you ask

  • Near me is every dish within 10 km of you, best first. Panning doesn't change it.
  • Move the map and a Search here button appears at the top. Tap it and the sheet becomes what's on screen, titled with the suburb (Carlton). The location button takes you back to Near me.
  • Titles say the place only: Near me, Carlton, or the restaurant you tapped. No "dishes".
  • Drag the sheet down and it minimises to its title, so the map is nearly full screen. It doesn't close completely, the same as Apple Maps, so the list is always one drag away.
  • Nothing nearby: one line, "No dishes within 10 km" (or "No dishes here" after Search here).

Zooming: dots far out, photos up close

  • Wider than about 25 km across (a whole city or more): dots only. Places close together share one dot, a little bigger the more it holds. Tap a dot to zoom into it.
  • Closer in: the best-rated places get photos while they fit; the rest stay dots. A dot that would touch a photo joins that photo instead, so a dot never sits on a picture.
  • As you zoom, one dot splits into several dots, then into photos.

The score pill's edge

The pill had a 2pt ring and the photo a 3pt one. Two ways to fix it: A. one 3pt ring round photo and pill alike, so the pin reads as one sticker; B. no ring on the pill, just a soft shadow. Recommended: A, because with B the butter pill blends into some photos (pastry, egg, curry).
