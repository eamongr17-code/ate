The rules underneath

New codesPescatarian (PESC), Halal (HALAL), Egg free (EF), Peanut free (PF), Shellfish free (SF).
What covers whatVegan covers Vegetarian, Pescatarian, Dairy free and Egg free. Vegetarian covers Pescatarian. Nut free covers Peanut free. A covered option is lit and faded in the picker, and counts toward that option on dishes.
DietsVegetarian, Vegan, Pescatarian, Halal, Dairy free. Labelled once 3 people mark the dish in 12 months with no veto, as today.
AllergiesGluten free, Egg free, Nut free, Peanut free, Shellfish free. Same pill and same 3-person rule as diets. The "?" popover ends with "Not a guarantee. Check allergies with staff."
Below 3 peopleNo pill and no sentence anywhere. This removes today’s "Ordered by 2 vegan eaters" line from the dish page and the place page. The count still shows in the "?" popover, and the place page filter still includes the dish.
Dairy freeStays a diet, because most people who pick it are lactose intolerant. It can move to Allergies if you'd rather.
Halal wordingThe label means halal eaters order it, not that the restaurant is certified. Same as Gluten free today.
