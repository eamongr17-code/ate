Paper Trail, by hand.

The same idea, receipts that haven't printed yet, redrawn in thick marker the way your references are: wobbly lines, hatching instead of fills, scribbles where the text would go, and a bit of mischief (a pencil with nothing to write, a fly on an empty rail, a lens pulling a face).

Two versions below: all ink, like your references, or ink with one small colour mark per drawing. My pick is all ink. It matches the references, and colour stays where DESIGN.md puts it, on scores and buttons.

When you're happy with it

How it ships

  • The empty state gains one drawing above its line; every screen keeps its copy and button.
  • The drawings ship as vector assets in a single ink colour, so dark mode flips them automatically.
  • For the real thing I'd have these drawn by hand (or traced from your sketches) rather than generated, so the wobble is a person's wobble. These set the scenes, the poses and the jokes.
  • DESIGN.md's "no illustration" rule for empty states gets updated to match.
