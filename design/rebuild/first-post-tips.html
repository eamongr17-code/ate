First post tips

Two one-time tips on a person's first entry, in the same style as the bell and Lists tips, plus the Save tip restored on the beta Feed. No new screens, no tour.

Phones are live size (iPhone 17 Pro). Tips are native TipKit popovers drawn with Ate's tip style. Each shows once ever, one at a time, and goes the moment its control is used or its ✕ is tapped.
