What happened and where sit on two lines with no photo. A tap anywhere opens the tag, which nothing on the row says. The X and the swipe do two different things. Every photo sitting is listed in full.

Proposed, light

One sentence per row: who, what, where, then the day in grey. The dish photo they took sits on the right. "Ate with" gets the same two pills as an invite: Decline and Add yours. A coral dot marks rows that arrived since you last looked.

Proposed, dark

Same page. The photos group shows the newest two sittings. The count and the chevron on its heading open all of them on their own page.

Swipe to clear

The glass X goes. Swiping a row left gives Clear: the row goes and the tag stays. That's the same as the X today. Decline moves from the swipe to the pill, and still asks once.

2. How the groups are split

A. By kind Recommended

List invites, Ate with, From your photos, as now. With a few rows each at beta size, the heading tells you what the pills will do before you read the row.

B. By time

Invites and tags in one list, newest first, split into New (unread) and Earlier, like Instagram and Reddit. From your photos stays its own group under them. This works better once people get dozens of notifications.
