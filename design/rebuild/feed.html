What changes from the current build

  • The custom header and the location chip go. "Feed" becomes the native large title, the city becomes its subtitle, and the chip's job moves to a pin in the trailing glass group. The custom compact header on scroll is replaced by the native inline title.
  • The Top Ate stops being a receipt. Receipts are what Ate prints from your words. The ranking becomes a photo-led chart: #1 a full photo card with its name large, #2 to #8 ranked rows. Every score gets the star token used everywhere else.
  • The area picker becomes a native Menu instead of the "Where?" sheet, with the same contents: Near me, Everywhere, then cities. It is one choice, so the contract makes it a menu.
  • Near me no longer jumps straight to a location read. The system prompt shows only after that tap; the Feed opens on your Journal's city, not "Everywhere".
  • Cravings get a door in the toolbar, a heart beside the pin, so you can change them from anywhere. The "Choose your cravings" row stays in the page only while you follow none.
  • The cravings sheet loses its top-right Done. Close sits top-left and the commit is one ink pill with a live count, "Follow 4 cravings", like every sheet that applies a choice.
  • The tab bar is the native one, quiet selected state, + in the detached slot, minimising as you scroll.
  • The Actions sheet adopts the sheet layout: close moves to the top-left, rows lead with their icons.

Decided

  • The Feed opens on your Journal's city.
  • Location is asked for only on Near me.
  • The selected tab is the quiet native state, no fill.
  • Section order, as built: The Top Ate, Because you loved…, New to the record, a shelf per craving, five latest receipts, then the end.
  • Because you loved… stays its own carousel, right after The Top Ate.
  • The Top Ate is Hero (chosen over Podium and Carousel): #1 a full photo card with no numeral, #2 to #8 ranked rows.
  • Shelves stay horizontal, as approved on 29 Sep.
  • Area is a Menu; cravings is a sheet; both sit in one trailing glass group.
