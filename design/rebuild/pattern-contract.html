Ate Pattern Contract

The cross-cutting rules every screen follows. A flow thread never decides these; it reads them. Where two flows disagreed, this page settles it. Eamon answered every open choice on 3 Oct.

One rule above all

Every piece of chrome is iOS 26's own: tab bar, navigation bar, toolbars, sheets, menus, search. Ate supplies tint, type and content inside them. No custom bar, frost, glass or header anywhere, in any flow, ever.

1. Tab bar

  • Native Liquid Glass tab bar: Journal · Feed · Search · You, Lucide icons, labels in Bricolage.
  • + is a tab (locked). It sits in the bar's detached trailing slot, the one iOS 26 gives a search tab, so it reads as its own glass circle while still being the system bar. It is plain glass with an ink plus, not coloured (Eamon, 3 Oct). Tapping it opens the composer sheet and never changes the selected tab.
  • Scroll down minimises the bar; scroll up restores it. Re-tap scrolls the tab to top.
  • The bar stays on every pushed page (locked for entries, now for all: place, dish, profile, settings, ratings, statement). It hides only under a sheet or the photo viewer.
  • Selected tab: the system's quiet state, a faint glass highlight with icon and label in ink. No solid fill, no tint colour. Nothing in the bar is coloured (Eamon, 3 Oct).

2. Top of screen: titles and controls

  • Tab roots use the native large title that collapses to inline on scroll, with the system scroll-edge effect over a ground-colour backing that fades out over 14pt, so body text never shows through the title or controls (Eamon, build 87). No frost, no compact header. The header wastes no space: the title and the glass group share one row. The title swap is a crossfade with no layout change, and the Journal | Saved switch stays pinned under the bar. The scroll-edge fade must be checked on a phone before a flow is called done: soft and even, never a band with a hard edge.
  • Titles: Feed, Search, You are their names. The Journal's title is the wordmark (never typeset) and its inline title on scroll is the month. Settled by Eamon on 3 Oct: the Journal shows the wordmark.
  • A root's controls sit trailing, in one glass toolbar group, at most three icons (Journal: photos, calendar, filter. Feed: area only. Search: filter. You: settings). Every chooser opens a sheet, including the Feed's area picker (Eamon, build 88); the only native Menu is the ••• overflow on pushed pages.
  • Pushed pages use the inline native bar: back, the page's name or byline, trailing controls as glass items. Overflow is a ••• Menu (Entry: Edit, Delete).
  • Subtitles use the bar's native subtitle (Feed: the city). No eyebrow text anywhere.

3. Sheets and modals

  • Everything modal is a native sheet with detents and the system grabber: composer (locked), place picker, filter, actions. Never a full-screen cover except the photo viewer and Welcome.
  • Corners: close is a glass disc, top left. The primary action is a glass disc, top right: the ink tick on the composer (locked), Share on the receipt. A sheet with no primary action has only the close disc. One layout, every sheet.
  • Sheet title: Bricolage 800, 30, left, under the corner row. Search field: 50 pill in the field colour.
  • A sheet that applies a choice ends in one ink pill at the foot with a live count ("Show 12 entries"). A sheet that picks one row closes on the tap, no button.
  • The composer's keys (Score, Place, Diet tags, camera, library) float in one glass capsule above the keyboard (locked). Diet tags is a key beside Score and Place (locked, not yet drawn).

4. Filters

  • One filter icon in the root's toolbar group opens one sheet holding every filter for that screen. The four standing chips are gone from all roots (Journal, Saved, Search). Settled by Eamon on 3 Oct.
  • While a filter is on, its chips show in a row under the bar (safeAreaInset top), each with ✕. No row when nothing is on.
  • Ranges (rating, date) are two-thumb sliders inside the sheet. Cities and areas are a native Menu.

5. Buttons and actions

  • Three kinds only: a glass disc (icon, 44) for corner actions; an ink pill (52, Bricolage 600) for a sheet's one commit; the bookmark on every dish row for Save. Nothing else is a button.
  • Coral appears only on the Share and Welcome grounds. No coral buttons, no big CTAs, no text links.
  • Destructive actions live in a ••• Menu with the system's red role and a confirmation dialog.

6. Empty, loading and photos

  • Empty: one anatomy app-wide. One 40pt Bricolage 800 line, centred between the header and the tab bar, and at most one ink pill 22pt below it. No receipt motif, no illustration, no second line.
  • Loading: skeletons of the real components, breathing to 45%. Never a spinner on first load.
  • Photos: squircle, radius 28% of side. The 3pt surface-colour ring is only for photos that overlap in a tilted cluster; a straight thumbnail never has a ring, and a letter tile matches the photo thumbnail beside it in size and radius. Photos never sit over a receipt; a cluster sits clear above the paper. Tilted clusters only on entry slips, the entry, the dish hero, Share and Welcome. Straight thumbnails in every other list. A dish with no photo is a letter tile, never grey.
  • Location is asked for only when the user taps Near me (Feed) or opens the place picker. Never on open. The Feed opens on the city of your Journal (Eamon, 3 Oct).

7. Paper, edges and the Journal's calendar

  • Paper is flat. One tone to the tip of the edge, no gradient (Eamon, round 3 and again 3 Oct). docs/DESIGN.md's "paper tone" ease to #F8F5F0 is stale; the build is right.
  • Edge B exactly. A 4pt wave under the paper, period fitted to the width so both ends match: P = W / round(W/12), y = 2.1 − 1.5·cos(2πx/P). On dish entry slips, the place menu and every receipt. Nowhere else.
  • No barcode on any receipt (Eamon, 3 Oct).
  • The calendar is a zoom level of the Journal, not a sheet. Months scroll vertically, never sideways. The calendar icon toggles list and calendar in one tap; each month heading carries a tappable year that opens the year view, and tapping a month there returns to it; 5.0 and secret-6 days stand out when zoomed out (Eamon, round 7 and 3 Oct).
  • History outranks the docs. Where docs/DESIGN.md or design/v1 disagree with the current build or Eamon's round feedback, the build and the feedback win.

8. Carried over from earlier rounds

Rulings Eamon already made that every flow keeps. The current build is the record; these are the ones a redraw is most likely to lose.

  • Receipts: the dishes lead and the place is fine print under a dashed rule; the restaurant name is never the hero; the receipt is straight once printed; dish rows and scores only, never a note under a line. Receipts are for what Ate prints from your words, not for rankings.
  • Scores: always the star token. An unrated dish is an empty slot. Aggregates print to one decimal with stars to the nearest half; an entry's average prints to two decimals. The secret 6 is a real score, in brick. Tapping a score pill opens the dish page everywhere except the composer.
  • Composer: the Diet key is a leaf icon beside the labelled Score and Place keys and unfolds into GF, DF, V, VG, NF. The tick stays disabled until a place is attached and shows stepping dots while posting. A place never appears inline in the words.
  • Dish page: reviews show avatar, handle and score only. "More to explore" and "More like this" sit under the reviews.
  • Search: one equal-width scope switch with the filter at its end, in the order Dishes, Places, People, Saved, opening on Dishes (Eamon, 3 Oct: "this is a dish focused app"). Before typing it shows recent searches and asks for no location. A place page is menu first with visits woven in.
  • You: the monthly statement is out of V1. A Ratings bar scrolls the page to its group; it never filters. Settings is the only corner control.
  • Feed: The Top Ate is a photo hero for the first dish with no rank numeral on it, then ranked rows 2 to 8 (Eamon, 3 Oct). Sections in order are The Top Ate, Because you loved, New to the record, craving shelves, latest receipts, then one quiet "What you follow" row at the end. The area picker offers Near me, Everywhere and cities. A slip with more than two dishes hides its words; the Journal shows the full caption.
  • Cravings are asked once (Eamon, 4 Oct): no heart, nothing in the corner. The first time only, one inline card after The Top Ate offers at most six categories; pick up to three or close it and it is gone for good. After that you follow from a category's own page (glass Follow toggle) and unfollow there or in What you follow.
  • A category page is editorial, like the Feed: hero dish with no numeral, ranked rows 2 to 8, Places known for it, New in the category, latest receipts, an end line.
  • Entry page: "More at" the place and "More like this" sit directly under the entry paper. The printed receipt screen centres the receipt and ends in one Share pill at the foot.
  • One dash style on a receipt. Hairlines run the full row width. An icon that only labels (Sort) must not use a glyph that opens something elsewhere. Every small photo on a slip, entry or receipt opens the one full-screen viewer.
  • Actions: Save, Share, Report and Block live in one actions sheet, the same everywhere.
  • Cards: a shelf card is a 16pt-radius photo or letter tile with the star token top left and a glass bookmark disc top right, equally inset, and text only beneath, flush with the image edge: name, then place. One card anatomy for every shelf and carousel in the app.
  • Photos open in the native zoom viewer. No " · " separators, no helper copy, no eyebrow text.
