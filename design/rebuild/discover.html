What changes

  • The heart and the cravings sheet go. The Feed's corner group holds only the pin. No wall of category pills anywhere in the app.
  • Follow moves to the category page. A glass Follow in the page's bar, Following when on. The Feed's shelves follow from it, in every option.
  • The "Choose your cravings" row goes. In A the shelves fill themselves; in C one capped card asks once; in B the rail is always there.
  • One list to manage. What you follow, reached from the end of the edition, replaces the picker: tap to open, swipe to unfollow, drag to reorder.
  • The category page becomes an edition. Hero and ranked rows like The Top Ate, then places, new, receipts and the end line. Not a list of rows.
  • The word "craving" leaves the interface. It is the category's own name everywhere: Curry, Dumplings, Vietnamese.
