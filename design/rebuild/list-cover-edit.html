List covers, and adding people to a list

Change a list's cover

Your list → ••• → Change cover. Pick one of the list's dish photos, or upload any photo and square it up. If you never pick one, the cover stays automatic: a mosaic of the first four photos, or the first photo when there are fewer. The second phone is live: tap the options. All four phones are real iPhone 17 Pro size.

2 · The cover sheet Live

The preview at the top shows exactly what the list will look like. Nothing is ticked until you pick; tapping your pick again unticks it and the preview goes back to the automatic mosaic. Tick saves; the close leaves the cover as it was. Upload from Photos opens the system photo picker, then screen 3. Dishes without a photo don't appear in the grid.

3 · Square it up

Your profile photo's crop screen, with a rounded square instead of the circle (the cover's own shape). Drag to move, pinch to zoom, tick to use it. The tick returns to the sheet with your photo in the preview and "Upload from Photos" checked.

4 · The cover everywhere

"Date night" now shows the uploaded photo on the shelf, on its page, and in the share link's preview. A chosen dish photo works the same way ("Best bao"). If that dish is later removed from the list, the cover goes back to the mosaic.

Add people to a list

Your list → ••• → Add people. Everyone who accepts sees the list in their own Lists view, with your name on it, and it updates as you change it. They can open it and share it, but only you can add, remove, reorder, rename or change the cover. That's the default I picked; letting them add their own dishes too is a bigger change and can come later. Each person gets an invite in their Notifications and the list only appears for them once they accept. They can leave it later from its ••• menu.
