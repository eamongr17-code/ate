What changes from the current build

  • You gets the native large title "You", and the settings gear moves from the header row into the toolbar as its one glass disc. The header keeps the build's 56pt avatar and one-line handle that truncates.
  • The histogram is the build's: an empty score is no bar at all, and an eleventh bar in the 6's brick appears only once a 6 has been given.
  • Top dishes stay tilted as the build draws them (-4, 3, -3, 4), side by side with no overlap and no ring. A dish with no photo is its letter tile at the same size and radius.
  • Ratings stays one stacked page (Eamon, 26 Sep): a bar scrolls to its group, never filters. The lit bar is drawn ink here; the build lights it coral.
  • Someone else's page moves the name into the bar (avatar, handle, city as the native subtitle). ••• opens the same Actions sheet as the Feed.
  • Settings becomes the system's inset grouped list, same rows, same order, with an icon before each label.
  • Welcome's receipt loses its barcode, like every receipt since 3 Oct.
  • Welcome's black button is Apple's own Sign in with Apple; the link keeps the build's words.
  • Handle loses its Continue button; the ink tick top right confirms, the same finish as the composer.
  • Share loses its Done and Share pills for the corner discs; the receipt is straight, the dishes lead and the place is fine print (rounds 5 and 6), no barcode.

Decided

  • The tab bar stays on Ratings, settings and someone else's page.
  • Pushed pages use the inline native bar with a glass back disc.
  • The selected tab is the quiet native highlight; + is a plain glass disc and nothing in the bar is coloured.
  • No social: no follow, no counts of followers. Save on each dish is the only action on someone's page.
  • Welcome and Share are coral grounds; the exported receipt stays light.
  • The monthly statement is out of V1.
  • Receipts print scores only, never a quote under a line; an unrated dish is an empty slot.
