Ate Pattern Contract

The cross-cutting rules every screen follows. A flow thread never decides these; it reads them. Where two flows disagreed, this page settles it. Eamon answered every open choice on 3 Oct.

One rule above all

Every piece of chrome is iOS 26's own: tab bar, navigation bar, toolbars, sheets, menus, search. Ate supplies tint, type and content inside them. No custom bar, frost, glass or header anywhere, in any flow, ever.

1. Tab bar

  • Native Liquid Glass tab bar: Journal · Feed · Search · You, Lucide icons, labels in Bricolage.
  • + is a tab (locked). It sits in the bar's detached trailing slot, the one iOS 26 gives a search tab, so it reads as its own glass circle while still being the system bar. It is plain glass with an ink plus, not coloured (Eamon, 3 Oct). Tapping it opens the composer sheet and never changes the selected tab.
  • Scroll down minimises the bar; scroll up restores it. Re-tap scrolls the tab to top.
  • The bar stays on every pushed page (locked for entries, now for all: place, dish, profile, settings, ratings, statement). It hides only under a sheet or the photo viewer.
  • Selected tab: the system's quiet state, a faint glass highlight with icon and label in ink. No solid fill, no tint colour. Nothing in the bar is coloured (Eamon, 3 Oct).

2. Top of screen: titles and controls

  • Tab roots use the native large title that collapses to inline on scroll, with the system scroll-edge effect. No frost, no compact header. The header wastes no space: the title and the glass group share one row. The inline title is centred. The scroll-edge fade must be checked on a phone before a flow is called done: soft and even, never a band with a hard edge.
  • Titles: Feed, Search, You are their names. The Journal's title is the wordmark (never typeset) and its inline title on scroll is the month. Settled by Eamon on 3 Oct: the Journal shows the wordmark.
  • A root's controls sit trailing, in one glass toolbar group, at most three icons (Journal: photos, calendar, filter. Feed: area, cravings. Search: filter. You: settings). A single-choice selector is a native Menu on its icon, never a sheet.
  • Pushed pages use the inline native bar: back, the page's name or byline, trailing controls as glass items. Overflow is a ••• Menu (Entry: Edit, Delete).
  • Subtitles use the bar's native subtitle (Feed: the city). No eyebrow text anywhere.

3. Sheets and modals

  • Everything modal is a native sheet with detents and the system grabber: composer (locked), place picker, filter, cravings, actions. Never a full-screen cover except the photo viewer and Welcome.
  • Corners: close is a glass disc, top left. The primary action is a glass disc, top right: the ink tick on the composer (locked), Share on the receipt. A sheet with no primary action has only the close disc. One layout, every sheet.
  • Sheet title: Bricolage 800, 30, left, under the corner row. Search field: 50 pill in the field colour.
  • A sheet that applies a choice ends in one ink pill at the foot with a live count ("Show 12 entries"). A sheet that picks one row closes on the tap, no button.
  • The composer's keys (Score, Place, Diet tags, camera, library) float in one glass capsule above the keyboard (locked). Diet tags is a key beside Score and Place (locked, not yet drawn).

4. Filters

  • One filter icon in the root's toolbar group opens one sheet holding every filter for that screen. The four standing chips are gone from all roots (Journal, Saved, Search). Settled by Eamon on 3 Oct.
  • While a filter is on, its chips show in a row under the bar (safeAreaInset top), each with ✕. No row when nothing is on.
  • Ranges (rating, date) are two-thumb sliders inside the sheet. Cities and areas are a native Menu.

5. Buttons and actions

  • Three kinds only: a glass disc (icon, 44) for corner actions; an ink pill (52, Bricolage 600) for a sheet's one commit; the bookmark on every dish row for Save. Nothing else is a button.
  • Coral appears only on the Share and Welcome grounds. No coral buttons, no big CTAs, no text links.
  • Destructive actions live in a ••• Menu with the system's red role and a confirmation dialog.

6. Empty, loading and photos

  • Empty: one 40pt Bricolage line on the ground and at most one ink pill. No receipt motif, no illustration.
  • Loading: skeletons of the real components, breathing to 45%. Never a spinner on first load.
  • Photos: squircle, radius 28% of side, 3pt ring in the surface colour when overlapping. Tilted clusters only on entry slips, the entry, the dish hero, Share and Welcome. Straight thumbnails in every other list. A dish with no photo is a letter tile, never grey.
  • Location is asked for only when the user taps Near me (Feed) or opens the place picker. Never on open. The Feed opens on the city of your Journal (Eamon, 3 Oct).

7. Paper, edges and the Journal's calendar

  • Paper is flat. One tone to the tip of the edge, no gradient (Eamon, round 3 and again 3 Oct). docs/DESIGN.md's "paper tone" ease to #F8F5F0 is stale; the build is right.
  • Edge B exactly. A 4pt wave under the paper, period fitted to the width so both ends match: P = W / round(W/12), y = 2.1 − 1.5·cos(2πx/P). On dish entry slips, the place menu and every receipt. Nowhere else.
  • No barcode on any receipt (Eamon, 3 Oct).
  • The calendar is a zoom level of the Journal, not a sheet. List, month, year, like a calendar app. The calendar icon and a pinch move between them; 5.0 and secret-6 days stand out when zoomed out (Eamon, round 7 and 3 Oct).
  • History outranks the docs. Where docs/DESIGN.md or design/v1 disagree with the current build or Eamon's round feedback, the build and the feedback win.
