Two layouts of the same rows. A is plain text, like today. B adds a line icon to each row, the pattern most settings pages on Mobbin use (Beli, Bluesky, Swarm, Nextdoor). I recommend B.

The settings page

Highlighted rows are new. "This iPhone" holds what lives on the phone, not the account: each row shows what iOS currently allows and opens Ate's page in the iPhone's Settings app.

Left out on purpose

  • Notification types. Ate only sends two pushes (someone tagged you, a list invite). Per-type switches need server work and aren't worth it for the beta; the Notifications row covers on and off.
  • Download my data, private account, email change, password. Not features Ate has. Sign in with Apple has no password.
  • Rate Ate. Waits until it's on the App Store.
  • A profile card at the top. The You tab already shows it; repeating it here adds scrolling.
