Map of nearby dishes

A map button on the Feed opens a full-screen map of what people have eaten around you. Pins are places that have dishes in Ate; the sheet lists those dishes. Two ways to draw it below, each in light and dark. Tap a pin on the live phones to see the place view.

Option A: photo pins and a dish sheet Recommended

Each pin is the best-rated dish photo at that place with its average score, the only badge on it. A place with more dishes shows a second photo stacked behind it (two photos at most); pins that overlap merge the same way. The sheet is titled Dishes near you, lists what's on screen best first, and updates when you pan; once you drag away from yourself it says the suburb instead (Dishes in Carlton). Tapping a pin turns the sheet into that place's dishes. It's the native iOS 26 pattern (Apple Maps, Find My), so the sheet and buttons are system glass.

Busy streets

Restaurants sit shoulder to shoulder, so photo pins can't all fit. The map shows the best-rated places as photo pins, only as many as fit without touching; every other place is a small butter dot. Zoom in and dots turn into photos as room opens up. A dot is still tappable: it grows into its photos and the sheet shows that place. The sheet always lists every dish in view, so nothing is hidden from the list. This is how Apple Maps ranks its own place labels, built into MapKit.

Option B: score pins and a card row

Pins are butter score pills. One card per place sits at the bottom; swiping the cards moves the selected pin. Lighter on the map, but you only see one dish at a time and the photos are hidden until you swipe.

How it holds up

  • Never empty. The map opens framed on you plus the nearest 10 places with dishes, however far (fourth phone above). With location off it opens on the city with the most dishes.
  • Cost. Apple Maps, built into iOS: free, no quota, no Google calls. The map adds nothing to the Places bill.
  • Volume. One query each time the map stops moving, for the visible area only, capped at 60 places, on the places location index that already exists. At 50 users that's a few hundred small queries a day.
  • Same data as the Feed. Only entries the Feed can already show; nobody's private journal appears.
  • Events. map opened, pin tapped, dish opened, place opened.
